## Visual conclusions

- The brand is a luxury private-club dark marketing identity, not a light SaaS or lime-fintech identity.
- Gold is the only high-emphasis conversion accent.
- Deep navy is a supporting panel color, not the page background.
- Ivory is used instead of pure white.
- Off-black is used instead of pure black.
- The appropriate motion level is restrained because financial education trust and compliance override spectacle.

## Production decision

The approved DCTS production Sales Page must preserve these verified tokens. Earlier light-and-lime prototype instructions are superseded. This audit does not authorize unverified logo, testimonial, result, scarcity, price-history, guarantee or access-duration claims.
