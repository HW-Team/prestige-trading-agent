# DCTS Sales Page UX and Conversion Brief

Status: Design planning
Source: `DCTS_Sale_Page_Final_Flawless.pdf` (20 pages)
Owner: CZ Nakinkul

## Source hierarchy

- The legacy DTCS website is the authority for branding, color, typography, and visual language.
- The supplied PDF is an input for copy, section order, and wireframe intent only.
- The PDF is not a visual-design or branding reference. Any visual treatment in it is non-authoritative.

## Design Read

Thai trading-education sales page for paid traffic and working adults who want a systematic routine. Preserve the verified legacy DTCS private-club identity: obsidian surfaces, ivory copy, Prestige gold accent, and restrained deep-navy panels. Mobile-first. Avoid neon crypto, casino, fake trading-terminal, and generic AI-fintech aesthetics.

### Design dials

- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 4
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 3
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 5
- Theme: single dark marketing theme inherited from the legacy DTCS site
- Shape rule: cards 14px, controls 10px, CTA pill only if used consistently
- CTA intent: `สมัคร DCTS` everywhere
- Verified palette: `#0F0F10`, `#F4F1EA`, `#C6A15B`, `#1D2A38`
- Verified typography: Trirong headings, Sarabun body, Montserrat labels/CTA, Playfair Display selected numbers

## Primary user

A Thai employee, business owner, or family provider who has traded before, feels time pressure, lacks a repeatable checklist, and is willing to pay for education plus ongoing support.

## Primary conversion

Paid ad or Messenger -> Sales page -> Understand DCTS -> Review evidence -> Accept offer -> Stripe checkout -> Verified payment webhook -> LMS enrollment and approved community access.

## Secondary conversion

Not ready to buy -> Ask the Messenger agent -> Receive FAQ and qualification -> Return to the same sales page or enter a lower-commitment beginner path.

## Recommended web page structure

### 1. Navigation

- DCTS logo
- หลักสูตร
- สิ่งที่ได้รับ
- รีวิว
- FAQ
- Primary CTA: สมัคร DCTS

Desktop nav stays one line. Mobile uses a simple menu plus persistent bottom CTA.

### 2. Hero

Job: state the transformation and show the product/coach immediately.

- Short headline, maximum two lines
- Subtext under 20 words
- One primary CTA
- One secondary action: watch the 3-minute explanation
- Real coach/product visual, not a fake dashboard
- No countdown unless it is connected to a real campaign deadline

Recommended message direction:

> เปลี่ยนการเทรดที่พึ่งอารมณ์ ให้เป็นกิจวัตรที่มี Checklist และเวลาจบงานชัดเจน

Do not lead with guaranteed income or a fixed weekly return.

### 3. Three-minute explanation

- Real VSL thumbnail and duration
- What DCTS is
- Who it is for
- What the viewer will learn from the video
- CTA repeated directly below the video

### 4. The current problem

Combine PDF pages 2-5 into one focused section:

- no entry checklist
- overtrading and decision fatigue
- no journal or statistical review
- chasing large wins instead of following risk rules

Use four distinct visual statements, not four identical generic cards.

### 5. The new operating model

Combine PDF pages 3, 6, and 9:

- Checklist before every trade
- Planned analysis window
- Risk and exit rules
- Daily Retest and journal
- Stop trading when conditions are incomplete

This section explains the mechanism without promising an outcome.

### 6. Evidence and methodology

- Redacted samples from Daily Retest
- Real journal screenshots
- Clearly defined date range and sample size
- Method used to calculate any statistics
- Past-performance disclaimer beside the evidence

No percentage or volume claim appears unless evidence has been approved.

### 7. What the learner receives

Combine PDF pages 10 and 13:

- five learning modules
- live teaching and recordings
- DCTS Master Checklist
- Trade Journal and Stats workbook
- case-study library
- community support

On mobile, use expandable module rows rather than a long wall of text.

### 8. Coach and support system

Combine PDF pages 11-12:

- real portrait of Coach Golf
- verified background
- real team photos or a documented team visual
- support channels
- response hours and expected SLA
- what Daily Retest support includes and does not include

### 9. Student evidence

Combine PDF pages 7 and 17:

- real video testimonials first
- short quotes, maximum three lines
- full name or approved public display name
- occupation only when consented
- no earnings quote unless documented and legally approved
- label individual results as non-typical when necessary

### 10. Offer and bonuses

Combine PDF pages 15, 16, and 18:

- current product name
- verified list price
- current selling price
- everything included
- bonus details
- access duration
- support period
- refund/cancellation policy
- Stripe checkout CTA

Scarcity and countdown must be driven by a real end date or real inventory. Do not reset on refresh.

### 11. Guarantee and risk

The draft contains a coaching-support promise. Keep it separate from trading outcomes.

Safe direction:

> หากทำตามบทเรียนและ Checklist ครบตามเงื่อนไข แต่ยังไม่เข้าใจกระบวนการ ทีมจะนัดทบทวนแนวทางการใช้งานให้ตามขอบเขตที่ระบุ

Must specify:

- eligibility
- required lesson completion
- required journal evidence
- request window
- what support is provided
- whether a refund exists
- what is not guaranteed

### 12. FAQ

Use accordions with approved answers:

- suitable experience level
- required platform knowledge
- live and replay access
- support schedule
- Daily Retest activities
- trial terms
- payment and invoices
- cancellation/refund
- access problems
- investment-risk disclaimer

### 13. Final CTA

- concise restatement of offer
- one `สมัคร DCTS` button
- verified checkout URL
- support link for unanswered questions
- no new claims introduced at the bottom

### 14. Legal footer

- Risk disclaimer
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Terms of Sale
- refund/cancellation policy
- business/legal entity details
- contact channel

## Copy claims that cannot ship without proof or revision

### Return and income claims

- `สร้างกระแสเงินสดสัปดาห์ละ 10%`
- `ทำกำไรสม่ำเสมอ`
- `กำไรหลักแสน`
- `สร้าง Cash Flow เสริมหลักแสน`
- testimonial claiming `กำไร 12%` and recovered course fee from the first trade
- any wording suggesting the learner can withdraw predictable income

Action: remove fixed-return language from the main promise. If historical examples are used, include complete evidence, date range, sample size, fees, drawdown, and a clear statement that results are not guaranteed.

### Absolute claims

- `ตัดอารมณ์ออก 100%`
- `ตรวจสอบผลแพ้-ชนะโปร่งใส 100%`
- `มั่นใจขึ้น 100%`
- `การันตีความมั่นใจและการดูแล 100%`
- `ไร้ความเสี่ยงพอร์ตแตก`

Action: replace with measured language such as `ช่วยลดการตัดสินใจตามอารมณ์`, `บันทึกและทบทวนผลตามกระบวนการ`, and `เน้นการบริหารความเสี่ยง`.

### Scale, authority, and history claims

These require an evidence folder before publication:

- 1,000+ traders/students
- 500+ trading days
- 1,000+ trade setups
- 100+ case studies
- 6 years of experience
- 2,000,000 THB market tuition/loss
- 15,000 screen hours
- 95% of traders
- 3-8 setups per day
- learn and trade within 3 months
- 80-90% reduction in screen time
- named community partners, academies, analyst, and certification labels

Action: verify each claim or remove it.

### Urgency and price claims

- 00:12:45:30 countdown
- only first 20 seats
- 89% discount
- list price 35,900 THB
- current price 3,990 THB
- bonuses valued at 15,990 THB
- lifetime access

Action: confirm price approval, actual list-price history, campaign end date, seat logic, bonus valuation basis, and access terms. Countdown must be server-driven and cannot reset per visitor.

## Mobile-first decisions

- Hero CTA visible in first viewport
- Sticky bottom CTA after the hero
- Video uses 16:9 and loads only when requested
- Course modules and FAQs collapse into accordions
- Offer card remains one column and never uses a dense pricing matrix
- Tap targets at least 44px
- No horizontal scrolling
- Thai body copy minimum 16px with generous line height
- Checkout handoff clearly states the secure payment destination

## Compliance and agent integration

- All checkout confirmation comes from Stripe webhook, never from chat text
- Paid LINE link is never embedded in page source or sent before verified approval
- Messenger agent can answer FAQ and route to checkout but cannot promise returns
- Analytics events: hero CTA, VSL play, curriculum open, testimonial play, FAQ open, checkout click, support click
- UTM parameters persist into checkout and CRM lead records

## Build recommendation

Create a mobile-first clickable mockup using the verified legacy DTCS brand, safe placeholder copy, and real asset slots. Approve the visual and evidence requirements before production coding. The production page should not publish unverified performance claims from the PDF.
